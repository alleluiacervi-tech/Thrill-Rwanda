# Thrill Rwanda dashboard and business workflow audit

## Staff implementation checklist

- [x] Give Admin, Sales and Operations a searchable enquiry contact directory with scoped case histories and links to follow up. Group by matching name, email and phone. Admins can also search direct bookings from the directory.
- [x] Add a case email composer for admins and assigned staff. Queue the email and activity together, use the case's email preference, record delivery attempts and direct replies to the configured company contact. Attempt delivery after submission and schedule daily queue recovery on Vercel.
- [x] Track supplier responses by hotel and hall so a second hall at the same property has its own status, layout, capacity and hold deadline.
- [ ] Restore production venue editing by applying the venue database migration and checking a published hall from enquiry through staff intake.
- [ ] Verify Resend delivery with the company sender and monitored reply address. Add signed delivery and bounce events, incoming replies linked to their cases, and frequent scheduled recovery for time-sensitive mail.
- [ ] Link verified client accounts to guest histories and bookings, add Sales and Operations handover with shared case access, and support staff-assisted bookings against the existing stock and pricing controls.
- [ ] Give authorized catalogue editors a shared location directory and draft publishing workflow for properties, halls, tours and cars.
- [x] Pilot flights with checked fare options and deadlines, passenger and ticket records, itinerary delivery and post-ticket changes through the ticketing partner. See [AIR_TICKETING.md](AIR_TICKETING.md).
- [ ] Pilot meetings with catalogue-linked rooms, time-specific holds, supplier confirmations, room blocks, deposits and a final organiser itinerary.
- [ ] Extend itinerary components to honeymoons, hotels, tours and safaris with room and permit confirmations, manifests, vouchers and change handling.
- [ ] Separate car hire and transfer activation, reserve vehicle capacity, assign drivers and record pickup, handover and return.
- [ ] Add wedding vendor milestones and change orders, and corporate and school group approvals, rosters and billing references.

The findings below describe the audited starting point. The first implementation passed 314 unit tests and 61 authenticated dashboard route checks. Local integration checks covered scoped client histories, concurrent email submissions, recipient preference, escaped email content and provider acceptance using a simulated transport. Production activation and supplier verification are tracked separately.

Thrill can sell an entire arrangement across its four product lines. A Kigali conference can include a hall, guest rooms, airport pickups, flights, and a post-event tour. A wedding can lead into a guest stay and a honeymoon. The dashboard already handles the enquiry, written quote, and finance stages. The largest gaps are customer correspondence, booking-specific work for each service, and the handover from Sales to Operations.

Keep two ways to sell. Use direct booking when Thrill or an approved partner can protect the stock, time slot, rate, and cancellation conditions. Use a written quotation when a supplier must check a live fare, venue hold, room block, permit, driver, or custom itinerary. Both paths should lead to a customer-visible confirmation and one place to request changes.

For a meeting, the customer can browse named halls and send one brief with dates, guest count, layout and room needs. Sales checks two suitable options and issues a proposal with scope and deposit terms. Operations places timed holds for the chosen hall, rooms and transport. Finance records the deposit, and the organiser receives one set of confirmed arrangements and a contact for changes. The coordination fee pays for that work, including confirmed alternatives if a supplier drops out under the agreed terms.

## Staff workspaces and daily tasks

| Task | Working entry point | Gap to close |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View and respond to clients | Sales and Operations open **Staff → Enquiries**. They can claim unassigned cases, set follow-ups, read permitted case history and record support work. Admin can view all cases, registered users and corporate accounts. | Give each customer a searchable profile spanning guest leads, bookings, enquiries, company roles and correspondence. Preserve explicit case access grants. A matching phone or email can suggest a duplicate for staff review. |
| Add a location, hotel, hall, tour or car | Admin uses **Catalogue**. Approved providers manage their own hotels, rooms, tours, cars and availability according to their categories. Hotels and wedding venues have location fields. | Add a shared **Locations** catalogue for destination, address and optional verified map pin. Attach locations to relevant hotels, halls, tours and pickup points. Add a scoped catalogue editor permission so staff can create and edit drafts with approval before publication. |
| Create a reservation on behalf of a client | Staff can capture an offline enquiry, quote it and record fulfilment and external confirmation. Customers create direct inventory reservations through the public checkout. Admin and provider dashboards handle existing bookings. | Add a staff-assisted reservation form that calls the same pricing, capacity and hold logic as checkout. Require a confirmed customer and authority to act, and capture the actual quote or booking terms. |
| Move a sale from Sales to Operations | Staff share a case workflow and Admin can assign ownership. Sales and Operations primarily see their own cases and unassigned cases. Finance has a scoped monetary view. | Give a case a sales owner and operations owner with explicit shared access and a handover task. Show what Operations is waiting for and by when. Keep the finance reconciliation and supplier-confirmation controls separate. |
| Confirm what has been purchased | Finance and Operations can record payment, supplier commitments and a case-level fulfilment reference. Direct bookings have their own status and inventory. | Give every trip or event component a supplier, hold deadline, price, reference, owner and completion status. Require evidence for each required component before issuing the final itinerary or confirmation. |
| Send an invoice link or a client reply | Finance can create, copy and revoke an invoice link. Staff can request automated email delivery with a quote. **Admin → Email templates** edits and previews templates. | Add **Send to client** for the current quote, invoice link, itinerary and confirmation. Give staff a message composer and correspondence timeline on the case, with separate internal notes and reviewed recipient permissions. |

The daily navigation should follow staff work. **My work** shows new and overdue enquiries, expiring supplier holds, replies waiting, departures and settlements requiring review. **Clients** lists people and companies with role-scoped case histories. **Bookings and trips** shows an itinerary and component checklist. **Catalogue** is where authorized staff manage content and rates. **Finance** and **Settings** keep their own permissions. The release checklist records company ownership, configuration and signoff. Put it under **Settings → Release readiness** for owners and admins, with an owner and next action for each blocked item.

Use the existing written-quote and accounting workflows for the first pilot. Customers can accept a quote, and Finance can create a payment link or collect through an approved method. Direct online collections currently support tours and vehicles in RWF. Properties approved for direct booking use pay at property. Enable online payment for a product once its inventory, currency, terms, refund workflow and supplier confirmation are covered end to end.

## Resend delivery and replies

Resend is already used for the quote and enquiry outbox, as well as existing best-effort booking and account mail. A new enquiry gets an email acknowledgement when its contact preference is email. An issued quote can be queued with the customer-facing snapshot. The queue records attempts, retries, configuration blocks, and provider acceptance. The template screen is a content editor and preview. It has no staff reply inbox.

The local environment lacks `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`. The checked Vercel schedule runs hold release and payment reconciliation. The enquiry-notification worker is configured in the VPS scheduler. Verify the scheduler actually serving the deployment before offering email delivery. The quote status “Quote sent” can be reached when a quote is issued, before Resend delivers it. Show separate labels for quote issuance, queued message, provider acceptance, delivery and bounce in the staff view. [Resend domain verification](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/domains/introduction) · [Resend webhook event types](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/event-types) · [Vercel cron plan limits](https://vercel.com/docs/schedules/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing)

Use a company-owned verified sending domain, a monitored reply mailbox and a recurring worker. Prove delivery to approved staging inboxes before customer use. Then add case-thread messages, send them through the existing outbox with stable idempotency keys, and store Resend's message ID. A signed webhook should update delivery and bounce states. Start with a monitored mailbox that staff can answer manually. Add inbound case threading through a dedicated reply address and Resend Receiving when staff are ready to handle unmatched replies. Limit messages to case-authorized recipients and deliver sensitive traveller documents through authenticated links. [Resend idempotency](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys) · [Webhook signature verification](https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/verify-webhooks-requests) · [Resend Receiving](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/receiving/introduction)

## Revenue model and rollout

| Offer | How Thrill can earn | What the dashboard must capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flights and concierge | Disclosed ticketing or servicing fee through an approved partner, plus approved margins on transfers and trip add-ons. | Partner cost, customer total, fee, fare deadline, issuance or dispatch evidence, and the reason a quote was lost. |
| Stays, tours and honeymoons | Contracted net rate or commission, plus a stated planning fee when it covers complex itinerary work. | Supplier rate and validity, customer-facing terms, package cost by component, gross contribution and any permit or property deadline. |
| Meetings, retreats and weddings | Planning or production fee for sourcing, holds, coordination, guest logistics and delivery. Add contracted commissions or margins where approved. | Event scope, hall and room holds, vendor cost, approved fee, milestones, deposit schedule and substitution or cancellation terms. |
| Corporate and group travel | Contracted per-trip fees or a management agreement after repeat usage and demonstrated service levels. | Company, arranger, quote acceptance, order or invoice references, allocations, response times and repeat bookings. |

Measure time to first human reply, conversion from qualified lead to accepted quote and completed booking, contribution after direct supplier cost and processing fees, refunds, and on-time fulfilment. Use the existing case and finance records as the starting point for these measures. Group results by product, offer and acquisition source. Keep currencies separate unless Finance approves an exchange-rate basis. The order below prioritizes complete workflows and existing distribution. It does not assume a measured revenue ranking.

## Verification scope

Local verification passed 313 unit tests, including three venue catalogue regression checks. Across Admin, Sales, Operations, Finance and an approved hotel provider, 58 authenticated page checks passed. The integration suites passed for flight intake, ownership, written quotations, finance, supplier fulfilment, client access, support, departure inventory and payment collections. Email and payment provider responses were simulated in those scenarios. Resend inbox delivery, live settlement, supplier inventory and ticket issuance require real deployment or partner evidence.

The public Vercel service returned HTTP 200 for the health endpoint, flights, honeymoons, weddings, hotels, cars, transfers, groups, corporate and Tembera u Rwanda. Events & Business returned HTTP 500 twice. The production runtime logs show Prisma `P2021` for the missing `business_venues` table. The repo migration `20261121120000_business_venue_management` creates that table and the linked rooms table. The local fix keeps the built-in directory available when the tables are missing and gives admins a clear message on the editor. Production still needs the migration and a guest-to-staff test using a published hall. Use the existing release procedure to match the app and migration database branches before deployment. The public status checks covered route reachability.
